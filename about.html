About Smoking Turf

Leading the industry in professional turf fumigation services

Our Story

Founded on the principle that healthy soil creates exceptional turf, Smoking Turf is at the forefront of professional turf fumigation services.

We began as a small team of soil specialists who recognized the critical importance of proper soil treatment in turf establishment. Today, we're trusted by industry leaders across the country.

Our commitment to innovation and environmental responsibility has led us to develop cutting-edge fumigation techniques that not only eliminate harmful pathogens but also preserve beneficial soil microorganisms essential for long-term turf health.
